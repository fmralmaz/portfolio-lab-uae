Investment Basics

Diversification

Spreading investments across different assets can help reduce the impact of one investment performing poorly.

Risk

Different investments have different levels of risk. Higher potential returns often come with greater uncertainty.

Compound Growth

Compound growth occurs when returns are added to an investment and future returns are calculated on the larger amount.

Asset Allocation

Asset allocation means deciding how much of a portfolio should be invested in different asset categories.
